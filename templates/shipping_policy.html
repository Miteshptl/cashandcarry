Shipping & Delivery Policy

OPERATIONAL HOURS: DAILY 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM

1. Service Availability & Pincodes

CnC Supermarket delivers groceries and household essentials across selected pincodes in Mumbai. Orders can only be placed for addresses within our active service zones (e.g., Pincode 400001). You can check instant availability using the location indicator in the website header.

2. Delivery Modes & Timelines

⚡ Instant Delivery Delivered within 20 to 45 minutes of order placement. Instant orders are accepted daily until the cutoff time of 9:45 PM.
📅 Scheduled Delivery Choose a preferred 2-hour delivery slot for same-day or next-day fulfillment at no extra delivery cost.

3. Delivery Charges

  • Instant Delivery Fee: Flat ₹29 per order.
  • Scheduled Delivery Fee: FREE for all eligible scheduled orders.
  • Delivery charges (if applicable) are itemized and displayed transparently at checkout before payment.

4. Order Status & Receiving

You can check the status of your order anytime in your account dashboard as it progresses from "Pending Acceptance" to "Out for Delivery". Please ensure someone is available at the specified delivery address to receive perishable items (e.g., milk, dairy, fresh vegetables) once your order is dispatched.

5. Unsuccessful Delivery Attempts

If our delivery partner is unable to reach you due to an incorrect phone number, incorrect address, or unattended delivery site, the order will be returned to our fulfillment hub. Perishable items may not be eligible for re-dispatch or refund in cases of customer unavailability.
