Refund & Cancellation Policy

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026

1. Order Cancellation Policy

At CnC Supermarket, we work quickly to pack and dispatch your daily grocery items. Cancellation terms depend on the status of your order:

  • Pending Acceptance: You may cancel your order free of charge anytime before store staff accepts your order.
  • Preparing / Out for Delivery: Once an order is accepted and marked as "Preparing" or "Out for Delivery", cancellations are no longer permitted due to the perishable nature of fresh food and grocery products.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

You are eligible for a partial or full refund under the following conditions:

📦 Damaged or Expired Goods If items delivered are spoiled, physically damaged, or past their expiration date at the time of arrival.
❌ Missing or Substituted Items If an item is marked out of stock or missing from your order and no acceptable substitute was provided.
🚫 Store Rejection If an order is rejected by the store due to operational constraints or stock unavailability.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Due to hygiene and safety guidelines, sealed personal care products, opened food items, and items damaged due to improper storage after delivery are non-refundable.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

  • How to Submit: Submit a request via our Refund Request Form within 24 hours of delivery. Please include your Order ID and photos of damaged goods.
  • Payout Method: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment method used via Razorpay or credited to your CnC Pay Wallet.
  • Processing Time: Bank/UPI refunds typically take 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your account once initiated.
